Knowledge & frequently asked questions

Technical guides and product-specific documents will be added here over time. Until then, request information directly.

How can I receive product technical information?

Send the product name and intended application through the contact form so available information can be reviewed.

How do I request a quotation?

Include your organisation, approximate quantity, delivery location and contact method. Pricing and supply terms are confirmed after review.

Are TDS and SDS files available for every product?

Only valid documents approved for the exact product are published. A sample file is never presented as an official document.

Can companies discuss business cooperation?

Share your organisation details and the proposed supply, distribution or procurement cooperation.

Are online purchasing and payments available?

The current website focuses on product information and commercial enquiries. Online checkout and payment are not active.

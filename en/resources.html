02 / FAQ
01What is PCE?+

Polycarboxylate ether is a family of polymeric water-reducing agents that disperse cement particles and are widely used in modern concrete admixtures.

02Is PCE the same as a ready-to-use superplasticizer?+

No. PCE may be the polymer/base material used in a formulation. A finished superplasticizer can include other components and formulation adjustments. Supply form and readiness for use must be confirmed for the exact grade.

03What affects PCE performance in concrete?+

Cement chemistry, supplementary cementitious materials, water-to-binder ratio, temperature, mixing time, addition sequence, transport, pumping and compatibility with other admixtures can all affect performance. Trials with actual project materials are essential.

04What information is needed for a quotation?+

Share company name, application, requested grade/specification, quantity and order frequency, destination city, packaging preference and contact details.

05Can PCE be used in engineered stone?+

It may be suitable in some engineered-stone formulations, but compatibility with resin/cement, pigments and fillers and the required surface quality must be evaluated through application trials.

06Is there one fixed dosage for all concrete mixes?+

No. Dosage depends on grade, raw materials, mix design and target workability. Start from the exact product instructions and determine the final dosage through trials.

07Which documents should accompany a product?+

At minimum, current TDS and SDS; where available, batch-specific COA, packaging specification, manufacturing/expiry date, storage conditions and use instructions.

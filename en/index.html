01 / SUPPLY FORM

Start by identifying
the form of PCE you need.

The term PCE alone does not define the supplied product. Base polymer, solution or concentrate, and ready-to-use superplasticizer must not be treated as interchangeable in composition, documentation or application.

01RAW MATERIAL

PCE base polymer / raw material

For buyers assessing the polymer as an input to their own formulation. Exact grade properties and process requirements must come from the relevant product documentation.

Include in an enquiry ↗
02SOLUTION / CONCENTRATE

PCE solution or concentrate

For enquiries where a liquid or concentrated form is preferred. Solids, density, stability, storage and any dilution requirement must be documented and confirmed.

Include in an enquiry ↗
03READY-TO-USE

Ready-to-use superplasticizer

For buyers requesting a finished admixture. Cement and aggregate compatibility, performance and dosage require valid TDS information and actual testing.

Include in an enquiry ↗

Important: Showing these three routes does not confirm availability or supply capability for all three forms. The supply form, manufacturer, grade and commercial terms must be confirmed for each enquiry. No dilution ratio or dosage is inferred from generic information.

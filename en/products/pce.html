02 / SUPPLY FORM

Three supply forms that must not be confused

A requested supply form is recorded as an initial preference. Final confirmation depends on exact grade identity, manufacturer documentation and supply feasibility.

SUPPLY FORM / 01

PCE base polymer / raw material

A base material for formulation. Its properties and handling must be taken from the exact grade documentation.

Initial customer preference; supply feasibility must be confirmed
SUPPLY FORM / 02

PCE-based solution or concentrate

A liquid form whose concentration, solids content, storage conditions and any dilution requirements are product-specific.

Initial customer preference; supply feasibility must be confirmed
SUPPLY FORM / 03

Ready-to-use superplasticizer

A finished admixture formulation. Application and dosage must follow the exact product TDS and validation trials.

Initial customer preference; supply feasibility must be confirmed
An important technical distinction: The term PCE alone does not tell you whether an item is a raw material, a solution/concentrate or a finished ready-to-use admixture. This distinction affects selection, safety, storage, dosage and supply conditions. No generic dilution ratio or dosage is suggested here. Dilution should only be considered when specified in the exact grade documentation and validated through controlled testing.

05 / FAQ

Questions to resolve before an enquiry

Answers intentionally avoid numerical or performance claims without grade-specific documentation.

Is PCE the same as a ready-to-use superplasticizer?

Not necessarily. PCE can be a base polymer or an ingredient in a formulation. A finished admixture has its own composition and specification.

Are dilution ratios or dosage values available here?

No generic ratio or dosage is stated. These values must come from the current TDS for the exact grade and controlled validation trials.

Can it be used for engineered stone?

This is a potential application, but suitability must be tested with the actual cement-based stone formulation and its surface and production criteria.

Which documents should be reviewed before purchase?

At minimum, the current TDS and SDS for the exact grade. Where supplied, the COA should be matched to the batch delivered.

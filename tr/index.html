01SPECIALIST POSITIONING

One defined focus.
A clearer route for industrial buyers.

Rather than presenting a scattered list of unrelated chemicals, PAK RAZ focuses its public catalogue on PCE-based materials and concrete admixture applications. Buyers should be able to distinguish a polymer raw material from a ready-to-use admixture, and review grade-specific data without unsupported claims.

02 / PRODUCT FAMILY

Polycarboxylate Ether (PCE)

A polymer used in the formulation of many concrete water-reducing admixtures and superplasticizers. Supply form, solids content, density, pH, compatibility and intended use must be verified for the exact grade using its TDS, COA and appropriate testing.

03 / APPLICATION PATHWAYS

Selection based on the real system,
not the chemical name alone.

Admixture performance depends on cement, supplementary cementitious materials, aggregates, mix design, temperature and transport time. The following application pathways require technical assessment and trials.

01

Ready-mix concrete

Assess grade compatibility with the actual cement and aggregates, workability retention and delivery conditions.

02

Precast concrete

Evaluate production requirements, cycle time, curing method and surface-quality expectations on the actual line.

03

Cement-based engineered stone

Only after compatibility trials with cement, fillers, pigments and the other formulation components.
